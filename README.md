# Skool Community Management Templates

Free templates, workflows, and automation examples for Skool community owners who want to improve onboarding, engagement, retention, and member activation.

This repository includes practical resources for:

- Skool onboarding flows
- Skool DM sequences
- Skool content calendars
- Skool post scheduling
- Member reactivation workflows
- Churn prevention systems
- Weekly community digest templates

These examples are designed for community owners who want to spend less time manually chasing members and more time building a sticky, active community.

## How to Use These Resources

1. **Browse** the templates and workflows above
2. **Copy** what is relevant to your community
3. **Customize** the language, timing, and triggers for your niche
4. **Implement** manually or with automation tools

These resources work for any Skool community regardless of niche, whether you run a fitness community, a coaching group, a SaaS community, or a creator membership.
