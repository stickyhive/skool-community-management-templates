# 7-Day New Member Activation Plan

A focused first-week plan for getting new Skool community members from "just joined" to "actively engaged" within 7 days. This plan combines DMs, content, and community touchpoints to maximize early activation.

---

## Why the First 7 Days Matter

Research across online communities shows:

- Members who engage in the first 48 hours are **3x more likely** to remain active long-term
- Members who never post in week 1 have a **70%+ churn rate** within 30 days
- The first meaningful interaction determines whether a member sees the community as valuable or forgettable

Your job in week 1: get them to experience value and take action.

---

## The 7-Day Plan

### Day 0 (Join Day): Immediate Welcome

**Timing:** Within 1 hour of joining

**Actions:**

1. Send welcome DM (personal, with a question)
2. Tag them in the welcome thread
3. Point them to the "Start Here" resource

**Welcome DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! Welcome to [Community Name] — really glad you are here.

I am [Your Name]. I want to make sure you get value immediately, so here is where I would start:

→ [Link to Start Here post or top module]

Also — what is your #1 goal with [topic] right now? I want to make sure I point you in the right direction.
```

**Success metric:** They reply to the DM.

---

### Day 1: Intro Post Prompt

**Timing:** 24 hours after joining (if no intro posted)

**Actions:**

1. Check if they posted an introduction
2. If yes: like it, comment thoughtfully, ask a follow-up question
3. If no: send nudge DM

**Nudge DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! Quick thought — dropping a short intro post is the best way to get connected with other members here.

Does not need to be long! Just:
- Who you are
- What you do
- What you are working on right now

Everyone is super friendly. Here is where to post: [link]
```

**Success metric:** They post an intro.

---

### Day 2: Value Delivery

**Timing:** 48 hours after joining

**Actions:**

1. Send a DM with a specific resource tailored to what they mentioned
2. Make sure they have access to the right classroom content

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! Based on what you shared about [their goal], I think you will really like this:

→ [Link to specific post/module/resource]

It covers [brief description]. Let me know what you think!
```

**Success metric:** They consume a piece of content.

---

### Day 3: Community Connection

**Timing:** 72 hours after joining

**Actions:**

1. Tag them in a relevant discussion
2. Or: connect them with a member who has similar interests/goals
3. Or: invite them to an upcoming live event

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! There is a great discussion happening right now that I think is relevant to you:

→ [Link to post]

[Member Name] shared something about [topic] that connects to what you mentioned. Would love to hear your take!
```

**Success metric:** They comment on someone else's post.

---

### Day 4: Participation Prompt

**Timing:** 96 hours after joining

**Actions:**

1. Create an easy win opportunity for them
2. Ask them a direct question in a post or DM
3. Lower the barrier to contribution

**DM (if still not posting publicly):**

```
Hey [Name]! We have a quick question thread going today — would love your input:

→ [Link to simple discussion prompt]

No expertise needed, just your honest experience. Even a one-liner is great!
```

**Success metric:** They make their first public comment (beyond intro).

---

### Day 5: Recognition

**Timing:** 5 days after joining

**Actions:**

If they have been engaging:
- Publicly acknowledge them (comment, shout-out, or like)
- Validate their contribution

If they are still quiet:
- Send a value-first DM with zero pressure
- Share an upcoming event or piece of content

**Public recognition example:**

```
Great point by [Name]! This is exactly the kind of insight that makes this community great.
```

**Success metric:** They feel valued and seen.

---

### Day 6: Deeper Engagement

**Timing:** 6 days after joining

**Actions:**

1. Invite them to go deeper (attend event, start a module, join a challenge)
2. Introduce a next-level commitment

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! You have been crushing it in the first week.

Quick heads up — we have [event/challenge/new content] coming up on [date] that I think would be perfect for you.

Here are the details: [link]

Would be great to have you there!
```

**Success metric:** They commit to a future engagement.

---

### Day 7: Week-1 Wrap-Up

**Timing:** 7 days after joining

**Actions:**

1. Send a wrap-up DM
2. Ask for feedback
3. Set expectations for what comes next

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name]! You have been in [Community Name] for a full week — how is it going?

A few quick questions:
1. What has been most valuable so far?
2. Anything you wish we had more of?
3. Any questions I can help with?

Also — here is what to look forward to next week: [brief preview]

Glad you are here! 🙌
```

**Success metric:** They reply with feedback, indicating investment.

## Activation Scorecard

Track each new member against these milestones:

| Milestone | Target Day | Weight |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Replied to welcome DM | Day 0 | ⭐ |
| Posted introduction | Day 1 | ⭐⭐ |
| Consumed first resource | Day 2 | ⭐ |
| Commented on another post | Day 3–4 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Participated in discussion | Day 4–5 | ⭐⭐ |
| Attended event or completed module | Day 6–7 | ⭐⭐ |

**Scoring:**

- 8+ stars by Day 7 = Fully activated (high retention likelihood)
- 5–7 stars = Partially activated (needs continued attention)
- Under 5 stars = At-risk (move to reactivation workflow)

---

## Scaling for Multiple New Members

| Community Size | Approach |
|----------------|----------|
| 1–5 new members/week | Fully manual, highly personalized |
| 5–15 new members/week | Template-based with personalization |
| 15+ new members/week | Automation with personal touchpoints |

For high-volume communities, the core DM sequence can be automated while keeping Day 3 (connection) and Day 7 (feedback) as manual, personalized touchpoints.

---

## Common First-Week Mistakes

1. **Only sending one welcome message** — it takes 5–7 touchpoints to activate someone
2. **Being too generic** — reference their specific goals or intro
3. **Overwhelming with content** — give one thing at a time, not 10 links
4. **Not following up on silence** — if they do not engage, nudge (gently)
5. **Expecting them to figure it out** — guide them step by step
