# 30-Day Skool Engagement Plan

A complete 30-day plan for launching (or relaunching) engagement in your Skool community. Follow this day-by-day to build momentum, establish posting rhythms, and create an active, thriving community culture.

---

## Who This Is For

- Community owners launching a new Skool group
- Owners relaunching engagement after a quiet period
- Anyone who wants to go from "dead community" to "active community" in 30 days

---

## The 30-Day Overview

| Week | Focus | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| Week 1 (Days 1–7) | Foundation | Establish posting rhythm, get first conversations going |
| Week 2 (Days 8–14) | Momentum | Increase member participation, create patterns |
| Week 3 (Days 15–21) | Depth | Deepen relationships, introduce recurring formats |
| Week 4 (Days 22–30) | Sustainability | Systematize what works, build habits |

---

## Week 1: Foundation (Days 1–7)

### Day 1: Set the Tone

**Post:** Welcome/relaunch announcement

```
Hey everyone! Starting today, I am committing to making this community the most valuable place for [topic].

Here is what you can expect:
- Daily posts designed to help you [outcome]
- Weekly [events/challenges/discussions]
- Direct access to me for questions

Let's kick things off: What is your #1 goal with [topic] right now? Drop it below.
```

**Also do:**
- Pin this post
- DM your 5 most active members asking them to comment first

---

### Day 2: Quick Win Post

**Post:** Share an actionable tip or framework

```
Here is something that took me too long to learn about [topic]:

[Insight/framework/tip]

The key takeaway: [one sentence]

Have you tried this? What has worked for you?
```

---

### Day 3: Engagement Prompt

**Post:** Ask a low-barrier question

```
Quick poll: Which do you struggle with more?

A) [Option A]
B) [Option B]

Drop your answer below and I will share what I have seen work best for each.
```

---

### Day 4: Member Spotlight

**Post:** Highlight a member (even if the community is small)

```
Shout out to [Member Name] who [achievement/contribution].

[Brief context about what they did and why it matters]

Drop a 🔥 to show them some love!
```

**If community is new:** Spotlight yourself or share a member's backstory.

---

### Day 5: Educational Content

**Post:** Teach something substantial

```
[Title: How to X in 3 Steps]

Step 1: [Explanation]
Step 2: [Explanation]
Step 3: [Explanation]

The biggest mistake people make: [common mistake]

Questions? Ask below.
```

---

### Day 6: Casual/Fun Post

**Post:** Something lighter to build connection

```
It is [Day] — tell me one thing going well for you this week.

Big or small, share it below. Let's celebrate together.

I will go first: [your win]
```

---

### Day 7: Weekly Recap + Plan

**Post:** Summarize week 1, preview week 2

```
📋 Week 1 Recap:

- We had [X] posts and [X] comments
- [Member] shared [insight]
- Top discussion: [topic]

Next week we are going deeper into [topic]. Get ready for [preview].

What was your biggest takeaway this week?
```

---

## Week 2: Momentum (Days 8–14)

### Day 8: Challenge Launch

```
7-Day Challenge: [Specific challenge related to your topic]

Every day this week, do ONE thing:
- Day 1: [Task]
- Day 2: [Task]
- Day 3: [Task]
- Day 4: [Task]
- Day 5: [Task]
- Day 6: [Task]
- Day 7: Share your results

Who is in? Comment "I'm in" below.
```

### Day 9: Behind-the-Scenes

Share something transparent about your process, business, or journey.

### Day 10: Q&A Thread

```
Open Q&A: Ask me anything about [topic].

No question is too basic. Drop it below and I will answer every single one.
```

### Day 11: Resource Share

Share a tool, template, or resource the community can use immediately.

### Day 12: Discussion Prompt

Ask a question that requires members to share experiences.

### Day 13: Member Win Celebration

Celebrate someone's progress from the challenge.

### Day 14: Week 2 Recap

Summarize, preview week 3, thank active members by name.

---

## Week 3: Depth (Days 15–21)

### Day 15: Deep-Dive Post

Long-form educational content. Your best insight. The kind of post that could be a blog article.

### Day 16: Hot Take / Contrarian Opinion

```
Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take about your topic]

Here is why I believe this...

[Explanation]

Do you agree or disagree? Let me know below.
```

### Day 17: Introduce a Recurring Format

```
Starting today: [Weekly Feature Name]

Every [Day] I will share [format description].

This week: [first edition]
```

### Day 18: Connect Members

```
Connection thread: Drop your [niche/specialty/location] below and tag someone you think others should know about.

Let's help each other!
```

### Day 19: Case Study

Share a detailed breakdown of a result (yours or a member's).

### Day 20: Accountability Thread

```
What are you committing to finishing by end of this week?

Post it below. We will check in on [Day].
```

### Day 21: Week 3 Recap

Note increased engagement, highlight top contributors.

---

## Week 4: Sustainability (Days 22–30)

### Day 22: Member-Generated Content

Ask members to share their own tips, frameworks, or wins.

### Day 23: Feedback Post

```
Quick feedback request: What would make this community even more valuable for you?

- More [type of content]?
- Different format?
- Specific topics?

Be honest — I am building this for you.
```

### Day 24: Live Event or AMA

Host a live call, AMA, or workshop.

### Day 25: Template or Framework Share

Give members something they can implement today.

### Day 26: Celebration Post

Celebrate the community's progress over 30 days.

### Day 27: Future Preview

Share what is coming next month. Create anticipation.

### Day 28: Repeat What Works

Look at your highest-engagement posts and do a variation.

### Day 29: Gratitude Post

Thank specific members for their contributions.

### Day 30: Monthly Wrap-Up + Next Steps

```
📋 30-Day Community Report:

- Total posts: [X]
- Total comments: [X]
- Active members: [X]
- Top contributor: [Name]
- Most popular post: [Title]

This is just the beginning. Here is what is coming next month: [preview]

Thank you for being here. This community is only as good as the people in it — and you all are incredible.
```

---

## Post-30-Day: Sustaining Momentum

After the initial 30 days:

1. **Establish a weekly content calendar** — use the [Skool Content Calendar Template](../templates/skool-content-calendar-template.md)
2. **Set up recurring formats** — keep what worked, drop what did not
3. **Batch content creation** — write a week of posts in one sitting
4. **Monitor engagement weekly** — track which post types perform best
5. **Delegate or automate** — bring in help for repetitive tasks

---

## Engagement Benchmarks

| Week | Target Comments/Post | Target Active Members |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Week 1 | 3–5 | 10–20% of total |
| Week 2 | 5–10 | 20–30% |
| Week 3 | 8–15 | 30–40% |
| Week 4 | 10–20 | 40%+ |
