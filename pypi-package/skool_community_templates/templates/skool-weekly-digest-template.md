# Skool Weekly Digest Template

A template for creating weekly community digest posts that summarize activity, celebrate wins, and keep members coming back to your Skool group regularly.

---

## Why Weekly Digests Work

Weekly digests serve multiple purposes:

- **Reward active members** by highlighting their contributions
- **Re-engage lurkers** by showing them what they are missing
- **Create FOMO** that drives members back into the community
- **Build a sense of momentum** and community growth
- **Give you a forcing function** to consistently review community health

---

## Weekly Digest Template

### Post Title

```
📋 Weekly Digest — [Date Range]
```

### Post Body

```
Here is everything that happened in [Community Name] this week:

---

🏆 WINS OF THE WEEK

- [Member 1] achieved [result]
- [Member 2] shared [insight/milestone]
- [Member 3] helped [Member 4] with [problem]

---

🔥 TOP POSTS

1. [Post title] by [Author] — [X comments]
2. [Post title] by [Author] — [X comments]
3. [Post title] by [Author] — [X comments]

---

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

[One sentence summary of the most valuable insight shared this week]

---

📅 COMING NEXT WEEK

- [Event/content planned for Monday]
- [Event/content planned for Wednesday]
- [Special announcement or challenge]

---

🙋 QUESTION OF THE WEEK

[Engagement prompt to drive comments on this post]

---

See you in the community! 💪
```

---

## Digest Sections Explained

### Wins of the Week

Highlight 2–4 member achievements. These can be:

- Results they shared in the community
- Milestones they hit
- Helpful answers they gave
- Content they created

This makes active members feel valued and motivates lurkers.

### Top Posts

Link to the 3 most engaging posts from the week. Choose based on:

- Comment count
- Quality of discussion
- Value delivered

### Key Takeaway

Distill the week into one actionable insight. This makes the digest valuable even for members who do not click through.

### Coming Next Week

Preview upcoming content. Creates anticipation and gives members a reason to come back.

### Question of the Week

End with a low-friction engagement prompt so the digest post itself generates comments.

## Variations

### Short-Form Digest (for smaller communities)

```
📋 This week in [Community]:

✅ [Member] hit [milestone]
📝 Best post: [title]
🎯 Next week: [preview]

What was YOUR win this week? Drop it below 👇
```

### Data-Driven Digest (for metrics-oriented communities)

```
📊 Community Stats — Week of [Date]

- New members: [X]
- Posts this week: [X]
- Comments this week: [X]
- Most active member: [Name]
- Engagement rate: [X]%

Top contribution: [summary]
```

---

## Tips for Effective Digests

1. **Post on the same day/time each week** — consistency builds habit
2. **Tag members you mention** — they will get notified and feel recognized
3. **Keep it scannable** — use bullets, bold, and clear sections
4. **Include links** — make it easy to click through to content
5. **Write it in under 15 minutes** — do not let this become a chore

---

## Automating Your Weekly Digest

Compiling the digest manually means reviewing the entire week's activity, noting top posts, tracking wins, and writing it up. For active communities, this can take 30+ minutes.

You can speed this up by:

- Keeping a running note throughout the week
- Bookmarking standout posts as you see them
- Using a community management tool to surface top posts and active members automatically
