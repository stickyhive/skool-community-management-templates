# Skool Onboarding Checklist

A step-by-step checklist for welcoming new Skool community members and guiding them from "just joined" to "actively engaged" within their first week.

---

## Why Onboarding Matters

Most Skool community churn happens in the first 7 days. Members who do not engage within their first 48 hours are significantly less likely to stick around. A structured onboarding process reduces early churn and increases lifetime value.

---

## The Onboarding Checklist

### Immediately After Joining (Day 0)

- [ ] Send a welcome DM introducing yourself and the community
- [ ] Point them to the "Start Here" post or classroom module
- [ ] Tag them in the welcome thread or create a welcome post for them
- [ ] Ask one specific question to prompt a reply (e.g., "What is your biggest challenge with X right now?")

### Day 1

- [ ] Check if they have introduced themselves
- [ ] If not, send a gentle nudge: "Hey, would love to hear a quick intro from you in the community!"
- [ ] Like/comment on their intro if posted
- [ ] Ensure they have access to all relevant resources

### Day 3

- [ ] Send a check-in DM: "How are you finding things so far? Anything I can help with?"
- [ ] Point them to a specific piece of content relevant to their stated goal
- [ ] Verify they have completed the first classroom module (if applicable)

### Day 5

- [ ] If still inactive: send a value-first DM with a tip, resource, or link to a popular post
- [ ] If active: acknowledge their contributions publicly

### Day 7

- [ ] Send a "first week wrap-up" DM
- [ ] Ask for feedback: "Anything you wish the community had more of?"
- [ ] Invite them to participate in an upcoming event or challenge

---

## Onboarding DM Templates

### Welcome DM (Day 0)

```
Hey [Name]! Welcome to [Community Name] 🎉

Super glad to have you here. Quick tip to get the most out of this:

1. Check out the [Start Here] post — it has everything you need
2. Drop an intro post so the community can get to know you
3. If you have any questions, just DM me

What is your biggest goal with [topic] right now?
```

### Check-in DM (Day 3)

```
Hey [Name], just checking in — how are you finding the community so far?

Is there anything specific you are looking for help with? Happy to point you in the right direction.
```

### Nudge DM (Day 7)

```
Hey [Name]! Just wanted to follow up — we have [specific event/content] coming up this week and I think you would get a lot out of it.

Let me know if there is anything I can do to help you get more value from the group!
```

---

## Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Welcome DM sent within 1 hour | 100% |
| Intro post within 48 hours | 60%+ |
| First comment/like within 72 hours | 70%+ |
| Completed first classroom module (Day 7) | 50%+ |
| Replied to at least one check-in DM | 40%+ |
